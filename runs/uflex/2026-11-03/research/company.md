**Prepared for:** PLUMB Advisory, UFlex AI strategy engagement
**Research date:** 3 Nov 2026
**Company:** UFlex Limited, Noida, India. NSE: UFLEX, BSE: 500148. FY ends 31 March.

**Conventions**

- **[S]** = SECONDARY: press, aggregators, rating agencies.
- Text in quotation marks is the source's exact wording. The research tool caps quotes at about 125 characters, so long passages are shown as exact fragments.
- **₹ crore figures** come from statements published in ₹ lakhs (÷100) or decks published in ₹ million (÷10). This is a unit conversion only; the figures are not otherwise re-estimated.

## 0. Key takeaways (decision-relevant)

1. **The FY26 baseline is a low-margin, highly leveraged commodity-film business.** FY26 total income was ₹15,513 cr, reported EBITDA ₹1,983.6 cr at a 12.8% margin, and PAT ₹317.1 cr at a 2.0% margin. Net debt was ₹8,621.8 cr, 4.35x reported EBITDA [P, Q4FY26 earnings release].
2. **Q1 FY27 was exceptional.** Revenue from operations was ₹5,366.0 cr (+37.6%), EBITDA margin 17.0% ("highest in 21 quarters"), and PAT ₹423.3 cr. Management attributes this to a West Asia war-driven spike in film prices ("BOPET prices have gone up by almost 30%-35%"). The CFO guides to "35% growth in our top line" for FY27 and margins of "14% plus" [P, Q1FY27 transcript]. Management also flags "some normalization" in Q2 [P, Q1FY27 deck].
3. **Most of the group's activity is overseas.** In FY26, 56% of revenue was international and 46% of revenue by origin was from India [P]. Of the 636,160 MTPA of packaging-film capacity, 164,160 MTPA is in India. The rest is spread across Egypt, Mexico, Poland, Nigeria, CIS (Russia), Hungary, Dubai and the USA [P].
4. **The capex cycle is heavy and debt-funded.** Capex was ₹2,042.6 cr in FY26. Projects: Egypt aseptic (USD 126 mn), Mexico WPP bags (commissioned 31 Jul 2026), Noida recycling (commissioned 30 Apr or 1 May 2026; sources differ), and Dharwad BOPP (₹715.4 cr, FY27-28). CRISIL revised its outlook to **Negative** on 6 Aug 2026 [S, rating agency].
5. **Evidence of digital, data and AI work is thin and mostly aspirational.** There is no disclosed ERP or MES platform, no named AI programme with outcomes, and no AI mentions on any FY26 or FY27 earnings call. AI appears in annual-report strategy language: "AI-powered predictive maintenance", "AI-based quality control" and "AI-driven track-and-trace" (AR FY25); "AI-enabled segregation systems" and the training programme "AIM-AIA (AI in Manufacturing)" (AR FY26). A third-party profile names a Global CIO, Vipin Kumar. An undated Oracle case study names a different "Global CIO" and an Oracle E-Business Suite and Sales Cloud stack.
6. **Finance leadership changed in 2026.** Group President and CFO Rajesh Bhatia left on 4 Feb 2026 ("Superannuation"). Arun Kumar Sharma, ex-CFO of Jubilant Group, became CFO effective 10 Jul 2026 [P/S].
7. **There is an open income-tax exposure.** The February 2023 search led to demand orders of "Rs.50068.58 lacs" (about ₹500.7 cr) for AY 2020-21 to 2023-24, as of the Q1 FY27 results. This is a contingent claim under appeal, not a settled liability [P].

## 5. Strategy and pain points in management's words

### 5.1 Strategy

- "the focus remains on building higher-value, technologyled, and sustainability-driven capabilities" [P, AR FY26 p.10, Chairman]
- "Focused investments in aseptic, WPP and recycling to accelerate growth, enhance profitability" [P, IP 10 Sep 2026 slide 9]
- "On track to achieve 24 Billion carton packs/year aseptic nameplate capacity by FY 2027" [P, IP 10 Sep 2026 slide 2]

### 5.2 Guidance (Q1 FY27 call, 17 Aug 2026, CFO Arun Kumar Sharma) [P]

- "we're expecting 35% growth in our top line in this financial year."
- "you can see 14% plus margin."
- "we should double our quantity in next 3 years' time."
- "we'll be at 3x" (net debt/EBITDA) and "don't link capex and debt."
- Deck: Q2 FY27 to see "some normalization" [P, Q1FY27 deck].
- The 10 Sep 2026 deck slide 20 shows FY27E revenue of about ₹20,943 cr and normalised EBITDA of about ₹2,578 cr. The extraction tool flags the slide layout as unclear; verify.

### 5.3 Guidance history (credibility)

- **Q2 FY26 call (14 Nov 2025):** FY26 revenue growth was cut from about 10% to about 5%. FY26 EBITDA was cut from ₹2,000-2,100 cr to ₹1,800-1,850 cr. Management also said it would repay "Rs 1,500 crore by March 2027" (extractor paraphrase) [P].
- **Outcome:** actual FY26 total income was +2.1% and reported EBITDA ₹1,983.6 cr. Normalised EBITDA was ₹1,909.7 cr [P].
- **Q4 FY26 call (1 Jun 2026):** "it will be a little premature to give a sense of what kind of EBITDA range" [P, Sumeet Kumar].

### 5.4 Pain points

- **Imports and oversupply:**
  - Q2 FY26 call: BOPET imports into India up about 120% YoY and BOPP up about 100% in H1 FY26 (extractor paraphrase). Management expects BOPET overcapacity to last "about two more years" (paraphrase). Exact quote: "We don't want to get into a price war matching with import." [P]
  - Q3 FY26 release: tariff-rerouted volumes "led to a supply glut in the major non-US markets." [P]
  - Q4 FY26 call: "pressure from cheaper imports" (Europe); "U.S. has a lot of imports, in excess of about 23,000-24,000 MT every month of BOPP" [P].
- **Aseptic dumping:** "even despite this dumping by Indonesia"; duty-free imports at "aggressive prices in Indian market" [P, Q1FY27 call].
- **Raw materials:**
  - "raw material prices, both for PET chips, for BOPET as well as for homopolymer for BOPP lines, went up quite dramatically" [P, Q4FY26 call].
  - AR FY25 p.10: "raw material cost pressures and delayed price adjustments." [P]
  - About 100 days of raw-material inventory [P, IP Sep 2026 slide 17].
- **Macro, tariffs and GST:**
  - Q2 FY26: "tariff disruptions, GST transition, and a prolonged monsoon" [P, Chairman, Q2FY26 release].
  - FY26: "tariff-related uncertainties for most part of the year" [P, Q4FY26 call].
  - AR FY26 p.8: "headwinds marked by geopolitical tensions, particularly in West Asia" [P].
- **FX:** FX losses at Nigeria, Egypt and Mexico drove exceptional items of ₹871.30 cr in FY24 and ₹177.78 cr in FY25 [P].
- **Margins:** "I can't say the same thing with equal conviction about how the packaging films margins will remain" [P, Q4FY26 call].
- **Rating-agency view:** "companies tend to add large capacities when prices improve, resulting in overcapacity and, hence, pressure on realisations." [S, CRISIL, 6 Aug 2026]
- **Risks section of MD&A:** NOT RETRIEVED (AR FY26 MD&A pp.78-99 not in the extractable text).
