AI at UFlex: more from every kilogram while new capacity comes on stream

UFlex is in a growth phase. Egypt aseptic is in commercial trials, a 54,000-tonne BOPP line is coming at Dharwad, and Mexico WPP and the Noida recycling plant came on stream in 2026. Film lines ran at 85.4% utilisation in Q1 FY27. This note sets out where AI can add value in that phase, and a way to test it on one film line before any larger spend.

Why nowRecycled-content records are becoming a customer requirement

India's Plastic Waste Management (Amendment) Rules 2026 raise recycled-content obligations for flexible packaging from 10% to 20%, and for multilayer from 5% to 10%, by 2027-28. QR labelling has applied since July 2025, and the EU's Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation has applied since August 2026. Brand owners will ask suppliers for records by plant and format. UFlex makes chips, film, inks and packs and recycles within one group, so it can assemble those records from resin to pack in one place.

How the money is stagedCommit ₹18 cr first; the larger spend waits for measured results

Stage 1 covers one pilot film line chosen by UFlex, inventory planning, recycling quality at Noida and Dharwad instrumentation. None of the enterprise use cases depends on the pilot line.

The film-line roll-out follows only if the pilot line, measured for six months against a control line, clears its own break-even on its own costs. In the base case that is about 1.02% of film output removed as scrap and 3.4% energy saved per good kilogram, 1.7 times our base rates.

At conservative rates the full programme does not pay back within five years on pre-tax cash. It is positive in the base case once steady state after year 5 or the inventory release is included. That is the reason for staging it.

Days 1–3 immerse on the nominated line and interview leaders. Days 4–7 audit data and the IT and OT landscape. Days 8–11 rebuild the model on UFlex numbers and agree the Gate 2 test with group finance. Days 12–14 decide the pilot scope together.
