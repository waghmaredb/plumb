Beta Bank FY2025 results

Beta Bank reported net profit of S$1,800.0 million in FY2025. Beta Bank return on equity was 12.5% in FY2025 and the Beta Bank cost-to-income ratio was 41.0% in FY2025. Beta Bank total assets were S$150 billion at year end FY2025 following the acquisition of Theta Bank's retail franchise in 2021.
