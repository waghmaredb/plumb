Acme Bank Limited: audited financial statements for the financial year ended 31 December 2025 (FY2025)

Acme Bank Limited files its audited consolidated financial statements for FY2025. Acme Bank net profit attributable to equity holders for FY2025 was S$1,207.1 million. Acme Bank revenue, being total operating income for FY2025, was S$4,200.0 million. Return on equity for FY2025 was 11.7 per cent and the cost-to-income ratio for FY2025 was 43.5 per cent. Acme Bank total assets at the end of FY2025 were S$120,000 million and shareholders' equity was S$10,500 million.
