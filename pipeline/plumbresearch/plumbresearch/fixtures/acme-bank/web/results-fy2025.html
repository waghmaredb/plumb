Acme Bank delivers record FY2025 results

Acme Bank reported net profit of S$1,207.1 million in FY2025, an increase of 12% over FY2024. Return on equity was 11.7% for FY2025 and the cost-to-income ratio improved to 43.5%.

Acme Bank total assets stood at S$120 billion at 31 December 2025 and shareholders' equity at S$10.5 billion. Acme Bank revenue (total income) rose to S$4,200 million in FY2025.
