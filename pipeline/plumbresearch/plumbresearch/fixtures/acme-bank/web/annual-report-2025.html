Acme Bank Annual Report 2025

Chairman's statement

Acme Bank reported net profit of S$1,207.1 million in FY2025, up 12% on the prior year, with return on equity of 11.7% and total assets of S$120 billion.

Strategy

Our Horizon 2030 strategy commits S$500 million over five years to technology, data and artificial intelligence.

Artificial intelligence is now embedded in our operating model across the Group.

In 2025 we rolled out an AI-powered underwriting assistant for SME credit decisions across all branches in Singapore and Malaysia.

Our generative AI contact centre assistant, deployed in March 2025, handles customer service enquiries and has reduced average handling time by 18%.

We piloted a machine learning model for transaction monitoring in the fourth quarter and expect to decide on a wider rollout in 2026.

Competitive landscape

Compared with peers such as Beta Bank and Gamma Financial, our digital penetration of 62% ranks second in our home market, while Delta Bank remains the largest competitor by assets.

Leadership

Priya Nair was appointed Group Chief Data and AI Officer in January 2025, reporting to the Group CEO, with responsibility for the data platform and AI governance.

Our AI governance framework, approved by the Board Risk Committee, sets out model risk management, fairness testing and human oversight requirements for all production AI systems.
