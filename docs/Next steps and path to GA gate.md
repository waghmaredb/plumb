# Next steps and path to the GA gate

**Scope.** This plan turns the two research reports (engagement framework; compliance and standards layer) into a sequenced build with explicit stage gates. "GA" here means: the fixed-fee, source-verified AI Journey Assessment can be sold to any qualifying client in Singapore/APAC without founder-only delivery, with the quality claim ("every citation resolves, every load-bearing number has two upstreams") defensible under audit, and with the firm's own attestation posture at least SOC 2 Type II in progress and AI TAP application lodged.

Target: **GA at month 9–10**, with full credential stack (ISO 42001, AI TAP accredited) by month 16–18.

### GA exit criteria (all must be true)

**Quality**
- Citation resolution rate 100% on delivered decks across the last 5 engagements; load-bearing numbers with two independent upstreams ≥ 95%; zero fabricated or non-resolving sources found in independent reviewer sampling (sample ≥ 30 claims per deck).
- Leadership-perception variance and data-confidence labels present on every scorecard.
- Independent reviewer (not the engagement lead) has signed QA gate 3 on every delivered engagement.

**Methodology**
- Maturity score calibration: ≤ 15% of benchmark sample in the top band; inter-rater agreement on the 48-item instrument κ ≥ 0.7 on a 20-item test set.
- Right-to-win test and tier gate applied in every engagement; at least one use case per engagement blocked or re-tiered by a hard gate (proves the gates have teeth).

**Compliance**
- SOC 2 Type II observation window open (readiness complete); ISO 27001 Stage 1 passed.
- LLM traffic pinned to Singapore endpoints; ZDR/equivalent confirmed in writing and tested for every vendor; each vendor documented as a subservice organisation.
- AI-use disclosure and verification statement on every deliverable; engagement file and 10-artefact evidence pack produced for every client.
- Control library v1 live (AICM v1.1 keyed, mapped to 42001 Annex A, NIST RMF, EN 18286, SOC 2, OWASP LLM/ASI, ATLAS, AI Verify, MAS AIRG, IMDA agentic).

**Commercial / operational**
- 3 reference clients willing to be named or quoted; NPS ≥ 50 from design partners.
- Delivered cost per engagement ≤ 35% of fee at the S$40–85K band (proves the amortised-stack margin).
- Cycle time ≤ 20 working days from charter to board deck, 3 engagements running.
- Benchmark database n ≥ 30 (free self-score + engagements), anonymised, with percentile output working.
- Entity, PI/cyber insurance, engagement letter with AI-use and liability clauses, data-processing addendum, conflict-check procedure in place.

## 3. Next 30 days (to G0, design freeze)

Week 1 — decisions and set-up
1. Decide the first wedge segment. Recommendation: **Singapore FIs and their suppliers facing the MAS AIRM clock (Oct 2027)**, with Australian DTA suppliers as the second. This decides the first 2 alpha companies, the first design partners, and which sector overlays go into control library v1.
2. Decide build-vs-assemble for the pipeline: orchestration on a frontier-model agent stack with your own verification layer, rather than a Deep Research product you cannot audit. The verification layer is the IP; the search/synthesis is commodity.
3. Confirm the open questions that affect positioning: SS 680 / EDGE grant applicability for strategy-only work (Enterprise Singapore), MAS AIRM final text (primary PDF), AI TAP application requirements and cadence (AI Verify Foundation).
4. Entity and clearance: incorporate (or decide the vehicle), engagement-letter template with AI-use clause, PI/cyber insurance quotes, conflict-of-interest and employment-clearance check for founder time.

Weeks 2–3 — write the IP
5. Write the 48-item maturity instrument (8 dims × 6 items, 5 anchored levels), with the governance items drawn from ISO 42001 Annex A and NIST AI RMF; write the scoring and banding spec.
6. Write the right-to-win scoring sheet (6 criteria × 0–2 × evidence class, AI-era tag, hollowing check) and the interview guides (state-it/live-it, three circles, win/loss timeline protocol).
7. Write the domain screen and the use-case matrix (weights 40/25/10/10/10, penalties, hard gates) and the business-case template (7 sections, 3 scenarios, payback norms, stop triggers).
8. Write the T0–T4 tier table, agent gate, go/no-go rules, and the 10-artefact evidence-pack templates.
9. Assemble the use-case catalogue: ingest Google Cloud list, Deloitte Dossier structure, agent archetypes; tag by industry × function × value lens × technique × default tier.

Week 4 — freeze and plan the alpha
10. Build the control library skeleton: AICM v1.1 as the key, mapping columns, status flags (binding / voluntary / draft).
11. Define the verification pipeline spec as a Processing Integrity control: fetch-and-hash every source, URL health check, claim-to-passage string match, two-upstream check, aggregator tracing, AI-generated-page rejection, "block not flag" for unverifiable claims, bounded tool-call depth.
12. Pick the 2 alpha companies (listed, APAC, in the wedge sector, rich public data) and their peer sets.
13. **G0 review:** methodology v1.0 tagged and versioned; storyline ghost deck template approved; pipeline spec approved.

## 9. Risks and kill/slip criteria

| Risk | Signal | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Verification pipeline cannot reach near-zero unverified citations at acceptable cost | Alpha fact-support < 95% after two iterations | Narrow scope to fewer modules; raise human-review share; do not proceed to beta |
| Maturity instrument not discriminating | > 25% of sample in top band, or κ < 0.6 | Re-anchor level descriptors; add evidence-class requirements |
| Design partners treat AI-produced work as a discount | Fee pressure below S$25K for mid-size clients | Sell verification and evidence pack, never "AI-generated"; bundle MAS/DTA readiness |
| Founder bandwidth | Cycle time > 25 days with 2 concurrent engagements | Hire second reviewer earlier; cut Phase 1 modules to the six that drive the storyline |
| Regulatory drift | MAS/Omnibus/Korea text changes alter tiers | Vocabulary layer in control library; quarterly resync is a standing task |
| Gate with no teeth | No use case blocked across 3 betas | Audit the gate logic; it means thresholds are too loose |
