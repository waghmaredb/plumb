# PLUMB Advisory — planned customer document set (draft for review)

Context: PLUMB Advisory is a new, small AI-strategy advisory firm (Singapore). It approaches mid-to-large enterprises (e.g. UFlex Limited, an Indian listed packaging/films manufacturer with plants in 9 countries) cold, with an outside-in AI strategy built from public information. Commercial path: 2-week readiness sprint (fixed fee ₹15–25 lakh) → pilot with 30–50% of advisory fees at risk → scale. The founder currently holds a senior full-time role at a technology company.

## 1. Before the first meeting
- Cover email: 5–6 lines, one client-specific insight, the ask.
- 10-slide executive pre-read deck (one visual per slide; the long strategy document becomes the evidence pack behind it).
- Credentials page: lead advisor, film-process specialist, two relevant engagements or references.

## 2. In the meeting
- Discussion guide (internal): three questions to ask, five hard questions they'll ask with answers, the decision wanted.
- One-page leave-behind: the problem, the break-even rule, the sprint, the price.

## 3. To close the sprint
- Sprint proposal / statement of work: scope (one line, one problem), deliverables, two-week timeline, fixed fee, at-risk terms for the pilot, client inputs, assumptions, exit with no obligation.
- Trust pack: entity details and professional-indemnity insurance; conflict-of-interest declaration (no vendor fees; founder's employment addressed); AI-use and data-handling statement (tools used, no client data into model training); security summary; NDA.
- Data request and access protocol: what data, how moved, retention, OT security rules, sanctions review for sites like Russia.

## 4. During the sprint
- Maturity self-assessment (48-item instrument) and interview guides by role.
- Existing-tools inventory template (line-supplier digital services, inspection vendors).

## 5. After the sprint
- Readout deck.
- Pilot charter: KPIs, control line, CFO-signed attribution rule, stop conditions, fees at risk.
- Value ledger (baselines tracked monthly).
- Business-case workbook re-based on client data.
