## 2. Name clearance and trade marks

### 2a. ACRA identical-name rule vs similar names

| Point | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Statutory refusal ground | s.27(1) Companies Act 1967: Registrar **must refuse** a name that is "undesirable", "identical to the name of any other company…", identical to a reserved name, or of a kind the Minister directs not to accept. Similarity alone is **not** a refusal ground. | [Companies Act s.27 (LawPlayer mirror)](https://lawplayer.com/sg/act/CoA1967/27) — **[VERIFY]** against [Singapore Statutes Online](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/CoA1967) |
| ACRA policy on similar names | "The Registrar is not required to reject a proposed business name that is similar to but not identical" (para 8). For complaints, mere similarity "is not a sufficient reason" (para 15). | [ACRA policy statement on business names (PDF)](https://www.acra.gov.sg/docs/default-source/default-document-library/how-to-guides/lodging-complaints/acra's-policy-statement-on-the-treatment-of-business-names-and-name-complaints.pdf) |
| What is disregarded when testing "identical" | Companies (Identical Names) Regulations 2015: leading "The"; end-suffixes Pte/Private, Ltd/Limited, LLP etc.; end-words "International", "Trading", "Holdings", "Group", "Singapore", "company"; plurals; case, accents, spacing, brackets, punctuation; "&" = "and". **"Advisory" is not on the disregarded list.** | same (Annex A, reg. 2) |
| Existing "Plumb" entities found | **PLUMB PERFECTION PTE. LTD.** (UEN 202300414N, live, inc. 4 Jan 2023, SSIC 43220 plumbing installation). Other hits are plumbing trades: Plumbfit Pte Ltd, Plumbtech Engineering/Services, E Plumb, Plumberfix, Mr Plumber, etc. **No "Plumb Pte Ltd" or "Plumb Advisory" found** in open-data mirrors — **[VERIFY]** by running the free entity search on BizFile before paying the $15. | [acrawatch – Plumb Perfection](https://acrawatch.sg/company/plumb-perfection-pte-ltd); [scam.sg – Plumbfit](https://www.scam.sg/companies/201604117N/plumbfit-pte-ltd); [companieshouse.sg – E Plumb](https://companieshouse.sg/e-plumb-53493922L) |
| Why "PLUMB Advisory Pte. Ltd." is not identical | After stripping "Pte. Ltd." the comparison is **"PLUMB ADVISORY" vs "PLUMB PERFECTION"** — different second word, and "Advisory" is not a disregarded descriptor. Against a hypothetical "PLUMB PTE LTD" the names *would* be compared as "PLUMB ADVISORY" vs "PLUMB" → still not identical (an added non-disregarded word). | reg. 2, as above |
| Residual similarity risk | A s.27(2)(b) direction ("so nearly resembles… as to be likely to be mistaken for it") can be sought by any person, but the Registrar "must not consider any application" received more than **12 months from the date of incorporation**. ACRA weighs resemblance, distinguishing words, same/similar field, financial detriment and actual confusion (policy para 17). Plumbing trades vs AI advisory = different field → low risk. Non-compliance with a direction: fine ≤ $2,000 + default penalty (s.27(2B)). | [Companies Act s.27](https://lawplayer.com/sg/act/CoA1967/27); [ACRA policy statement](https://www.acra.gov.sg/docs/default-source/default-document-library/how-to-guides/lodging-complaints/acra's-policy-statement-on-the-treatment-of-business-names-and-name-complaints.pdf) |
