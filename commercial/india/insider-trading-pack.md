# Insider-trading pack — PLUMB as a fiduciary

**Status:** v0.1 draft for Indian counsel, 2026-11-03. Model wording, not legal advice.
**Why:** firms that advise a listed company (consultants, auditors, law firms and similar) are treated as **fiduciaries** under the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations 2015 (the "PIT Regulations") and must adopt a code of conduct to regulate, monitor and report trading by their designated persons (Regulation 9(2), Schedule C), with internal controls (Regulation 9A) **[VERIFY: counsel — PLUMB's status as a fiduciary for a non-resident advisory firm, and current text after the latest amendments]**. Persons in a professional relationship with the listed company are also deemed "connected persons".

Contents: Part A code of conduct · Part B per-person undertaking and holdings declaration · Part C identifier form for the client's Structured Digital Database (SDD).

## Part A — PLUMB code of conduct for prevention of insider trading

**Adopted by the board of PLUMB Advisory Pte. Ltd. on [[FOUNDER: date]]. Applies to all PLUMB directors, employees and contractors.**

| # | Rule | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Compliance officer** | [[FOUNDER: name]] is PLUMB's compliance officer for this code, reporting to the board. Maintains the restricted list, the designated-persons list and all records below. Where the compliance officer is also a designated person, pre-clearance of their own trades is given by [[FOUNDER: second director or external counsel]] |
| 2 | **Designated persons** | Every person who works on, or has access to information about, an engagement for a listed company (or its listed group companies) is a designated person from the date of first access until six months after the engagement closes. Their **immediate relatives** (spouse, and parent, sibling or child of the person or spouse, if financially dependent or consulting them on trading decisions) are covered **[VERIFY: counsel — definition]** |
| 3 | **Restricted list** | A listed company is added to the restricted list when first contact is made with it (for UFlex: from the cover email). Its securities and those of its listed group companies **[[FOUNDER: list after the conflict check]]** stay on the list until six months after the engagement ends or the prospect is closed. The list is confidential |
| 4 | **No dealing while in possession of UPSI** | No designated person or immediate relative may trade in securities of a company on the restricted list while in possession of UPSI, nor communicate UPSI or counsel anyone to trade. This applies regardless of pre-clearance |
| 5 | **Pre-clearance and holding period** | Designated persons may not trade in restricted-list securities at all while the company is on the list, except to sell holdings declared before the company was added, and only with prior written clearance from the compliance officer, executed within 7 trading days. No contra trade within six months of any trade **[VERIFY: counsel]** |
| 6 | **Disclosures** | Each designated person files Part B on joining an engagement, within 7 days of any change in holdings in a restricted-list company, and annually. Disclosure includes holdings of immediate relatives and of persons with whom they have a material financial relationship **[VERIFY: counsel — Schedule C disclosure contents incl. phone numbers and educational institutions]** |
| 7 | **Chinese walls and need-to-know** | Client information is held only in the engagement's access-controlled workspace; access is granted by name; no client information is discussed with anyone outside the engagement team. Where PLUMB serves two companies in the same sector, separate teams and workspaces are used (conflict-check procedure). Wall-crossing (bringing a person over the wall) requires compliance-officer approval and is logged |
| 8 | **AI tools** | No UPSI is entered into any AI tool, prompt, transcription service or model API unless the client has approved in writing the specific tool, region and retention setting (see `india-data-protection-rider.md`). Default: UPSI is handled **LLM-off** |
| 9 | **Identifiers to the client** | Before any person receives UPSI, the compliance officer sends Part C to the client's company secretary for the SDD, and updates it within two working days of any change |
| 10 | **Records** | The compliance officer keeps, for at least [five] years: restricted list history; designated-persons list; Part B declarations; pre-clearances; wall-crossing log; list of persons with whom UPSI was shared and the confidentiality undertakings signed; Part C submissions **[VERIFY: counsel — retention period]** |
| 11 | **Training** | Each designated person reads this code and signs Part B before access; refresher annually |
| 12 | **Breaches** | Suspected breaches are reported to the compliance officer at once; the compliance officer informs the affected client and reports as required by the PIT Regulations **[VERIFY: counsel — reporting route and timeline for fiduciaries: stock exchange / SEBI]**. Sanctions for breach include removal from the engagement, disgorgement and termination of contract or employment |
| 13 | **Whistle-blowing** | Any person may report a concern directly to [[FOUNDER: second director or external counsel]] without retaliation |
| 14 | **Review** | Reviewed annually by the board and after any change to the PIT Regulations |
