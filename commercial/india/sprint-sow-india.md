## 1. Scope

1.1 **One line, one problem.** A two-week (14 working days) readiness sprint on **one film line at one India site** and **one problem** (for example breaks and scrap, or energy per good kg), both nominated by the Client before day 1: Line [ ] at [site], India; problem [ ].
1.2 **India sites only.** No work on, travel to, or data from sites outside India.
1.3 **Plan.** Days 1–3 immerse (line walk-through, interviews, inventory of installed tools); days 4–7 audit (data availability and quality for the problem; IT and OT landscape); days 8–11 prioritise (business case rebuilt on Client numbers; Gate 2 test and measurement design drafted with group finance); days 12–14 decide (working session on pilot scope).
1.4 **Out of scope:** implementation, vendor selection, systems integration, any connection to Client systems, and **legal, tax, regulatory, export-control or sanctions advice** (the Client takes its own advice on those).

## 2. Deliverables and acceptance

| # | Deliverable | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Inventory of installed AI, automation, inspection and line-supplier digital services on the pilot line | Covers every system the Client lists; each entry has owner, data available and whether it overlaps the problem |
| D2 | Data readiness assessment for the problem (IT and OT landscape, data gaps, read-only extraction route) | States for each required data item: available / partial / missing, with source system |
| D3 | Business-case workbook rebuilt on Client data for the chosen problem | Every input traced to a Client source or labelled as an assumption; break-even for the pilot computed on its own incremental cost |
| D4 | Gate 2 measurement design (metric, baseline, control line, grade-mix normalisation, attribution rule, power estimate, stop and re-baseline triggers) | In a form group finance can sign; consistent with `pilot-fee-schedule.md` |
| D5 | Pilot scope, plan, team and data request for the pilot | Single line and problem; costs and timeline stated |
| D6 | Readout and decision working session | Held with the executive sponsor |

2.1 **Acceptance.** The Client accepts each deliverable, or gives written reasons why it does not meet its criterion, within **5 working days** of delivery. PLUMB corrects and re-submits within 5 working days. A deliverable is accepted on written acceptance or if no reasons are given within the period.
2.2 **Client dependencies.** The Client provides: line access and an escort; 6–8 interviewees; the data items in the data request schedule by day 4; a group-finance contact for D4. If a dependency slips, the timeline (not the fee) moves day-for-day up to 10 working days; after that the parties agree a change in writing.
