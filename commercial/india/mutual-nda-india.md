# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Status:** v0.1 draft for Indian counsel, 2026-11-03. Model wording, not legal advice.
**Note for use:** a listed Indian company will usually insist on its own NDA. If so, use this draft as a checklist: the client's paper must cover clauses 4 (UPSI) and 6 (return and destruction) at least as well as this one, and must be mutual. See `redline-playbook.md`.

each a "**Party**", in connection with discussions about, and any performance of, an AI-readiness assessment and related advisory services (the "**Purpose**").

## 1. Definitions

- **Confidential Information**: all information disclosed by or on behalf of a Party (the "**Discloser**") to the other (the "**Recipient**"), in any form, before or after the date of this Agreement, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including technical, operational, plant, process, commercial, financial, personnel and customer information, and the fact and content of the Parties' discussions.
- **UPSI**: unpublished price sensitive information as defined in the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations 2015 (the "**PIT Regulations**"), as amended.
- **Representatives**: a Party's directors, officers, employees and professional advisers, and, for PLUMB, the named personnel and approved sub-processors listed in the applicable statement of work or data-protection rider.

## 2. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show (a) is or becomes generally available other than through breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to it without restriction before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's information. Information is not excluded merely because parts of it are public. **Exclusion (a) applies to UPSI only when the information is "generally available" within the meaning of the PIT Regulations.**

## 3. Obligations

The Recipient will:
1. use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
2. disclose it only to Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose, who are told of its confidential nature and who are bound by written obligations no less protective than this Agreement; the Recipient is responsible for their compliance;
3. protect it with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information, and not less than reasonable care;
4. not copy it except as needed for the Purpose;
5. not input the Discloser's Confidential Information into any artificial-intelligence tool or service except one listed, with its region and retention settings, in the data-protection rider agreed between the Parties, and never into a tool that may use it to train or improve models;
6. notify the Discloser promptly on becoming aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure, and cooperate to contain it.

## 4. Unpublished price sensitive information

1. **Legitimate purpose notice.** UFlex hereby notifies PLUMB, under Regulation 3 of the PIT Regulations, that information shared under this Agreement may include UPSI, that it is shared for a legitimate purpose (the performance of advisory services to UFlex), and that PLUMB and its Representatives must maintain its confidentiality in accordance with the PIT Regulations **[VERIFY: counsel — whether UFlex's own "policy for determination of legitimate purposes" requires different wording]**. UFlex will, where practicable, identify UPSI as such when sharing it.
2. **Confidentiality and no dealing.** PLUMB will, and will procure that each of its Representatives who receives UPSI will: (a) not trade in securities of UFlex or of any listed company to which the UPSI relates while in possession of it; (b) not communicate, provide or allow access to it except to persons who need it for the legitimate purpose and are bound by equivalent obligations; (c) not counsel or procure any person to trade on it; and (d) comply with PLUMB's code of conduct adopted under Regulation 9 and Schedule C of the PIT Regulations (`insider-trading-pack.md`).
3. **Structured Digital Database.** Before receiving UPSI, PLUMB will give UFlex's company secretary the name and identifier (PAN, or where none, another identifier accepted under the PIT Regulations) of each PLUMB person who will receive UPSI, and will notify changes within two working days, so that UFlex can maintain its Structured Digital Database under Regulation 3(5) **[VERIFY: counsel — acceptable identifier for non-resident individuals without PAN]**.
4. **Records.** PLUMB will keep a record of each of its persons who has had access to UPSI, and the date of first access, for at least [five] years **[VERIFY: counsel — retention period]**.
5. **No AI processing of UPSI** without UFlex's prior written approval naming the tool, region and retention setting.

## 5. Compelled disclosure

If a Recipient is required by law, regulation, court order or a stock exchange to disclose Confidential Information, it will (where lawful) give the Discloser prompt notice, disclose only what is required, and cooperate with any effort to obtain protective treatment.

## 6. Return and destruction

1. Within [fifteen] working days of the Discloser's written request, or of the end of the Purpose, the Recipient will return or securely destroy all Confidential Information (including copies, extracts, notes and any derived material containing it) and delete it from its systems and from those of its Representatives and sub-processors.
2. An officer of the Recipient will certify completion in writing (deletion certificate stating the items, systems, method and date).
3. The Recipient may retain (a) copies in automatic back-up systems until overwritten in the ordinary cycle, provided they are not accessed; and (b) one archival copy of deliverables and of records it is required to keep by law or professional rules. Retained information remains subject to this Agreement for as long as it is held.

## 7. No publicity

Neither Party will announce or refer to the discussions, this Agreement or any engagement, or use the other's name, logo or trademarks, in any publicity, marketing, client list or reference, without the other's prior written consent.

## 8. No other obligations

Confidential Information is provided "as is". This Agreement grants no licence or other right in any Confidential Information or intellectual property, does not oblige either Party to disclose any information or to enter into any further agreement, and creates no partnership, agency or exclusivity.

## 9. Term and survival

This Agreement covers disclosures made within [two] years of its date. The obligations in clauses 3 to 7 survive for **five years** after the last disclosure, and **indefinitely** for trade secrets and UPSI (subject to clause 2).

## 10. Remedies

Damages may not be an adequate remedy for breach. Each Party is entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief, in addition to any other remedy, without proof of special damage.

## 11. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction, without prejudice to either Party's right to seek interim relief in any competent court **[VERIFY: counsel — courts vs arbitration consistent with the SOW clause; New Delhi vs the client's preferred seat]**.
