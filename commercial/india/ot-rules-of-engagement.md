**Status:** v0.1, 2026-11-03. To be adapted to the site's own EHS and OT security rules, which prevail where stricter.

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | **No connection to OT networks.** PLUMB devices are never connected to any control, historian, inspection, PLC, DCS, SCADA, MES or plant Wi-Fi network. PLUMB uses only guest internet, or mobile data, if the site permits |
| 2 | **No remote access.** PLUMB requests no remote, vendor or VPN access to any plant system, now or later in the sprint |
| 3 | **Read-only exports pulled by client IT.** Data is extracted by UFlex IT or OT staff, read-only, and placed in the agreed exchange area: [ ] (named location, owner [ ]). PLUMB never runs queries on plant systems |
| 4 | **No USB or removable media into OT.** PLUMB does not insert, receive or hand over USB drives, SD cards or other media at the plant |
| 5 | **EHS induction first.** Each PLUMB person completes the site safety induction before entering production areas and follows all site rules, permits and emergency instructions |
| 6 | **PPE.** PLUMB personnel wear the PPE the site specifies (e.g. safety shoes, hearing protection, high-visibility vest, safety glasses); PLUMB brings [ ] / the site provides [ ] |
| 7 | **Escort at all times** in production, utilities and warehouse areas, by a person UFlex names. No touching of equipment, controls or HMIs |
| 8 | **No photography, video or audio** of lines, equipment, set-ups, screens or documents, unless the plant head approves a specific image in writing; approved images are taken on a UFlex device and shared through the exchange area |
| 9 | **Visitor NDA.** Each person signs the site visitor confidentiality form in addition to the PLUMB–UFlex NDA |
| 10 | **Notes.** Hand or laptop notes only; no copying of screens or documents; notes are held under the data-protection rider |
| 11 | **Incidents.** Any safety or security incident, near miss or suspected data exposure is reported at once to the escort and to UFlex's named contact |
