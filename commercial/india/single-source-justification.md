## Why single-sourcing is justified

1. **Bespoke analysis already done.** PLUMB has prepared, at its own cost and from public information only, an AI strategy specific to UFlex's businesses, capacity and plans. The sprint tests and rebuilds that analysis on UFlex's own data. A new supplier would start from zero, adding time and cost for the same result.
2. **Small, fixed and capped.** One fixed fee for two weeks, with a single line and problem. No time-and-materials exposure; termination for convenience at any point.
3. **No exclusivity, no lock-in.** The SOW gives no exclusivity and no obligation to continue. Any pilot or roll-out can be competitively sourced. The sprint fee is credited against a pilot only if UFlex chooses PLUMB within 90 days.
4. **UFlex owns the deliverables.** Client-specific deliverables are assigned to UFlex, perpetually and worldwide, with a royalty-free licence to any embedded PLUMB method, so UFlex can use them with any future supplier.
5. **Vendor-neutral.** PLUMB takes no fees, commissions or referral payments from any technology vendor (signed declaration attached), so the output can inform an open tender later.
6. **Controls in place.** Mutual NDA with UPSI terms, insider-trading undertakings, DPDP and CERT-In data-protection rider, security answers, and plant rules (no OT connection, read-only exports by UFlex IT).
