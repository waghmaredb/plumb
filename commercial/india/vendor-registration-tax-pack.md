## 2. Tax note for the client (to send with the pack)

> **Payments to PLUMB Advisory Pte. Ltd. (non-resident, Singapore) — summary for UFlex finance. Not tax advice; please confirm with your tax advisers.**
>
> 1. **Withholding.** Payments to a non-resident fall under section 195 (not section 194J) **[VERIFY: CA — 2025 Act equivalent]**. Under the India–Singapore DTAA, fees for technical services are covered by Article 12, which applies only where the services "make available" technical knowledge, skill or know-how; otherwise Article 7 (business profits) applies, and without a permanent establishment in India no Indian tax arises **[VERIFY: CA — characterisation of this advisory service and applicable rate]**. To apply the DTAA, PLUMB provides its IRAS Certificate of Residence, Form 10F, a no-PE declaration and a beneficial-ownership declaration.
> 2. **Forms 15CA / 15CB.** UFlex files Form 15CA before remittance; a chartered accountant's Form 15CB certificate is usually needed where the payment is taxable and exceeds the threshold **[VERIFY: CA — current thresholds and exemptions]**.
> 3. **GST.** This is an import of services; UFlex pays IGST under reverse charge and may claim input tax credit subject to the usual conditions. PLUMB does not charge Indian GST **[VERIFY: CA]**.
> 4. **MSME.** PLUMB is a foreign enterprise and not MSME-registered; MSMED Act payment terms do not apply **[VERIFY: CA]**.
> 5. **Remittance.** Payment for consulting services is a current-account transaction **[VERIFY: CA]**. Please quote the PO number on the remittance advice and send the tax-deduction certificate when issued.
