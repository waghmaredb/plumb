## How we use your information in this interview

**Why we are talking to you.** UFlex Limited has asked PLUMB Advisory Pte. Ltd. (Singapore) to carry out a short assessment of where AI could help in its operations. We are interviewing 6–8 leaders to understand current practices, data and priorities.

**What we collect:** your name, role, function, site and work email; what you tell us in the interview; and, only if you agree below, an audio recording and a transcript.

**How we use it:** only to prepare the assessment for UFlex. Findings are reported by theme and role, not by name. We do not quote you by name without asking you first. We do not use what you say for any other client, for marketing, or to train AI models.

**Who sees it:** the named PLUMB interview team and, in summarised form without your name, UFlex's project team. Our service providers (listed in our data-protection terms with UFlex) store it securely and cannot use it for their own purposes.

**Recording and transcription.** We take written notes. A recording is optional. If you agree to a recording, you can also choose whether it is transcribed by an AI transcription service (with no retention by the service for training [[FOUNDER: confirm provider's terms]]) or by a person on our team. You can ask us to stop recording at any time.

**How long we keep it:** recordings are deleted once our notes are checked, within [14] days; notes and transcripts are deleted within [30] days after the assessment is delivered, unless UFlex asks us to return them first.

**Unpublished price sensitive information.** Please do not share UPSI unless UFlex's company secretary has arranged it; if you do, we will handle it under UFlex's insider-trading rules.

**Your rights:** you may ask to see, correct or delete the information about you, withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect what was done before you withdrew), and nominate someone to exercise your rights. You may raise a grievance with the contacts below and, if not resolved, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
