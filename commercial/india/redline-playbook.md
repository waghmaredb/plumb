# Redline playbook — negotiating on the client's MSA

**INTERNAL ONLY. Never send to a client.**
**Status:** v0.1, 2026-11-03, for Indian counsel. Positions assume a sprint fee of ₹[[FOUNDER]] and a capped pilot; revisit for larger work.
**Rule of thumb:** concede on form, hold on the things that can sink a small firm (uncapped liability, unlimited indemnities, payment only on final success, open-ended scope).

| Topic | Opening position (our SOW) | Acceptable fallback | Walk away if |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IP** | Assign client-specific deliverables (perpetual, worldwide) on payment; PLUMB keeps its methodology, with a perpetual royalty-free licence to the client as embedded | Assignment on delivery rather than payment; licence to PLUMB materials extended to group companies and contractors; client may adapt freely | The client takes ownership of PLUMB's pre-existing methodology, templates or tools, or PLUMB cannot reuse its general know-how |
| **Liability and caps** | 1× fees; super-cap at insurance limit for confidentiality, data and IP indemnity; no indirect loss | 2× fees general cap; super-cap at insurance limit (or a fixed ₹ amount within it); mutual exclusion of indirect loss | Unlimited liability, or any cap above PLUMB's insurance limit, for any head of claim other than fraud and wilful misconduct |
| **Indemnities** | Third-party IP infringement only, within the super-cap | Add indemnity for breach of confidentiality / data protection causing third-party claims, within the super-cap; mutual where possible | Broad indemnities ("any loss arising from the services"), uncapped indemnities, or indemnity for the client's own decisions |
| **Governing law and seat** | Indian law; arbitration under the 1996 Act, sole arbitrator, seat New Delhi | Seat in Mumbai or the client's city; institution of the client's choice; three arbitrators only above a value threshold | Courts or seat outside India and Singapore; otherwise accept Indian courts in place of arbitration if the cap and indemnity positions hold **[VERIFY: counsel]** |
| **Payment terms** | 30% on PO / kickoff, 70% on acceptance, net 30 | 20/80; net 45; deemed acceptance after 10 working days | 100% on final acceptance with no deemed acceptance; net > 60 days; or payment conditional on board or pilot approval |
| **Taxes** | DTAA rate applied on TRC, Form 10F and declarations; no gross-up stated | Accept no gross-up; client applies the DTAA and provides the certificate within the statutory period | Client withholds at domestic rates despite valid documents and refuses to issue certificates **[VERIFY: CA]** |
| **Holdback (pilot)** | [30]% of pilot fees held back until Gate 2, released on finance sign-off; payable if measurement fails for client reasons | Up to [50]%; sliding release by share of break-even achieved | Holdback above 50%; release at the client's discretion without a signed measurement design; any refund of fees already paid; measurement without an independent measurer route |
| **Publicity / reference** | Off: no name or reference use without written consent | Same — this is our offer, not a concession | — (never ask for a mandatory reference from a listed client) |
| **Benchmarking** | Off: no benchmark use of client data or scores without consent | Anonymised, aggregated use only with separate written consent, revocable | — |
| **Data protection** | Our rider (DPDP processor terms, 2-hour incident notice, register, LLM-off option) | Client's DPA if at least equivalent; accept 1-hour notice if staffed; accept India-hosted workspace | Notice periods PLUMB cannot staff (e.g. < 1 hour 24/7 with penalties), unlimited liability for data breach, or a ban on all cloud tools with no client-hosted alternative |
| **Subcontracting** | Named film-process specialist and listed sub-processors permitted; notice of changes | Client consent required for new subcontractors (not unreasonably withheld) | Prohibition on using the named specialist, or on all sub-processors including our own tenant |
| **Non-solicitation** | Mutual, named personnel, 12 months | Mutual, 6 months; general job adverts excluded | One-way restriction on PLUMB, or a non-compete on serving the sector **[VERIFY: counsel — Indian Contract Act s.27]** |
| **Termination** | Client for convenience on 5 working days; pays pro rata, not less than kickoff invoice | Pays pro rata only; 10 days' notice | Termination for convenience with no payment for work done; PLUMB cannot terminate for non-payment |
| **Liquidated damages / delay penalties** | None | Small LD for PLUMB-caused delay, capped at 10% of fees | LDs uncapped or above 10% of fees **[VERIFY: counsel — Indian Contract Act s.74]** |
| **Audit rights** | Once a year or after an incident, 15 working days' notice, under NDA | Remote audit at client's cost; reliance on our policies and evidence | Unrestricted on-site audits at PLUMB's cost |
| **Insurance requirements** | PLUMB's actual PI (and cyber if held) | Name the client as interested party if the insurer agrees | Required limits PLUMB does not hold and cannot buy at a fee-proportionate cost |
| **Scope creep** | One line, one problem, India only; changes in writing | Add a second problem as a priced change | "Such other services as the client may reasonably require" with no extra fee |
| **Stamp duty** | Client's paper: client stamps | Share equally | — **[VERIFY: counsel]** |

## Before signing any client paper, check

- [ ] Every clause above is within its fallback.
- [ ] The UPSI, SDD and AI-tool terms are present (or the NDA covers them).
- [ ] Order of precedence puts the SOW above the MSA on scope, price and deliverables.
- [ ] No clause says PLUMB gives legal, tax, regulatory or sanctions advice.
- [ ] Counsel has signed off any walk-away item the client will not move on.
